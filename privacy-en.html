Publisher and scope

Rute Rasa is managed by Ardy Rute Rasa. Privacy contact: lynn21.gamemoba2@gmail.com. This policy version dated 30 October 2026 covers the local Android 1.9.0 test APK. Public internet accounts, revenue advertising and real-money purchases are not enabled in this package. The policy will be updated to match those services before activation.

Local play and device data

Guest mode stores progress, levels, stars, game coins, collections, equipment, courier choice, language and settings on the device. It does not create a server account or sync progress to another device. Game maps and addresses are fictional, rather than player GPS. Clearing app data or uninstalling can remove guest progress.

Age groups and children

The game targets ages 6 and up; an official store rating has not been issued. The app asks for an age band without a date of birth and stores it only on the device. Ages 6–17 enter local guest mode. In this version, Google/email entry, registration, recovery, account sync and account-based multiplayer are held for under-18 or unknown ages. An age choice is not identity verification or parental consent. Parent-supported child accounts are not available.

Reminders and permissions

Daily reminders are optional and initially off. If enabled, Android may request notification permission and schedule a reminder around 18:00 device-local time. Delivery can be delayed by the system. Reminder preferences and dispatch markers stay on the device and are not uploaded as account progress. Reminders can be disabled in Settings.

Accounts and online services

This local test APK is not configured to a public account service. If a separately configured test account service is used, registration may process a player name, email and password hash; Google entry may return the permitted identity, email and name. Sessions and progress support authentication, sync, recovery and security. The actual enabled providers, processing locations and retention must be specified before that service launches. Guest progress remains separate from server accounts.

SDKs, advertising and purchases

The app includes Google Identity, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads and UMP integrations. An SDK or test ad ID does not guarantee the absence of SDK processing. When an SDK service is requested, IP address, diagnostics, interactions and permitted identifiers may be processed according to configuration and provider policies. Revenue ads and real-money purchases remain disabled; this page does not claim those services are ready. Final SDK disclosures and privacy choices must match the released version.

Email support

If you contact lynn21.gamemoba2@gmail.com, the publisher receives your sender address, message and any attachments through the email service. They are used to answer the request and, where necessary, verify account ownership. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or government identifiers. Support information is retained as needed to resolve the request, protect security or meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion of data no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

Guest progress remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is removed. The publisher cannot restore local progress that was never synchronized. For accounts on a configured service, verified deletion removes email, name, password hash, provider links and cloud progress and revokes sessions. Limited pseudonymous transaction, migration and anti-replay records may remain to resolve rights/refunds or prevent duplicate grants; pseudonymous is not the same as anonymous. Public-service and backup retention must be finalized before activation. Request deletion.

Choices and updates

You can choose local guest play, review the device age group in Settings, change language/audio/reminders and contact the publisher about data. Updates will appear here with a new version date. This policy does not claim an issued store rating, store approval or parental consent.
